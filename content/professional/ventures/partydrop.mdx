title: PartyDrop
summary: Text-native ticketing platform with an autonomous AI agent.
date: 2026-04-01
---

A text-native ticketing platform — no app, no browser, the whole flow happens
over messages. Handling 11,500+ messages from 850+ users and generating
$5,000+ in revenue.

- Deployed an AI agent that autonomously handles identity verification, Q&A,
  and Stripe payments via tool calls.
- Engineered a real-time message delivery pipeline with batch processing,
  retries, and idempotency.
- Built an operator console to recover stuck agent conversations via DB-state
  edits; diagnosed and fixed the downstream failure, cutting manual
  intervention from 20% to under 1% of messages.
