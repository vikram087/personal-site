Co-founded Linkkup (April 2025 – June 2026), a platform for meeting people and building friendships, based in Davis, California. Shipped iOS, Android, and web apps featuring real-time chat with Discord-style community channels, events, shared media albums, user profiles, and a recommendation system for connecting people with overlapping interests. Grew the platform to 1,000+ users at UC Davis and 250,000+ views across Instagram and TikTok.

Live at [linkkup.ai](https://linkkup.ai).
