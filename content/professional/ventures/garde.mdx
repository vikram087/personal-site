Co-founded GARDE (October 2023 – April 2025), an AI fencing coach built out of Davis, California and part of UC Davis Plasma '25, developed in collaboration with Olympic medalists and USA Fencing. Built the platform's core infrastructure end to end — standing up the database, working across the React frontend and Flask backend, and integrating VLMs and CNN models for automated fencing analysis. Also engineered an HLS video encoding and streaming pipeline on object storage to serve match footage for review and coaching.

See more at [gardeai.com](https://gardeai.com).
