As a Software Engineer (January 2024 – January 2025), led architecture and development of MatSearch, a low-latency semantic search engine over materials science papers. Applied NLP to automatically categorize papers and built a hybrid search pipeline combining dense vector similarity with keyword matching, layered with faceted filtering and custom sorting so researchers could narrow results by category, date, and relevance. Containerized the full stack with Docker for reproducible infrastructure setup and deployment.

As a Frontend Developer (May 2023 – January 2024), built and maintained the
institute's site at [icam-i2cam.org](https://www.icam-i2cam.org/).
